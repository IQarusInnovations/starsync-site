StarSync — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 August 2026

The short version: StarSync collects nothing, transmits nothing off your network, and has no servers. The rest of this page spells that out.

What StarSync does

StarSync watches your own Starlink dish and router, on your own local network, and notifies you before a firmware update reboots them. It communicates only with two addresses on your LAN: 192.168.100.1 (the dish) and 192.168.1.1 (the router). It makes no other network connections of any kind.

Data collected

None. StarSync has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs that collect data. We (the developer) receive nothing from your device, ever.

Data stored on your device

StarSync keeps a small amount of data locally, solely so it can do its job:

  • A log of update and reboot events it has observed (device name, firmware versions, timestamps), used to show your history and estimate future reboot times.
  • Your settings, including the reboot hour you may have told the app.

This data never leaves your device. It is excluded from Android's cloud backup. It participates in Android's direct device-to-device transfer if you migrate to a new phone, which is a local copy between your own devices. Uninstalling the app, or using its "Clear history" and "Reset watch state" controls, deletes it.

Data shared with third parties

None. There is no mechanism in the app by which data could be shared: it has no connection to any server.

Permissions

  • Notifications — the app's entire purpose is to warn you before a reboot.
  • Local network access (Android 17+) — required to reach your dish and router. This is the only network access the app has.
  • Alarms & reminders (optional) — lets warnings arrive at the right minute rather than being delayed by battery optimisation. The app works without it, less precisely.
  • Run at startup — resumes watching after your phone restarts.

Children

StarSync is a network utility intended for adults who administer a Starlink connection. It is not directed at children and, collecting no data, collects no data from children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of StarSync ever changes what data is handled — for example, adding an opt-in feature that requires any collection — this policy will be updated before that version ships, and the change will be called out in the app's update notes. The effective date above always reflects the current version.
