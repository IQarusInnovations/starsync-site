IQarus — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 25, 2026

The short version: IQarus collects nothing about you and has no servers. The only time it reaches the internet is through Google Play, to sell the one-time unlock. Everything the app knows about you stays on your phone. The rest of this page spells that out.

What IQarus does

IQarus is an offline vocabulary app. Its entire dictionary is bundled inside the app, so it works with no connection at all. IQarus is free for its first 7 days; after that, a single one-time purchase through Google Play unlocks it for good. The app's only network access comes from Google Play's billing library, which talks to Google Play to show the price of the unlock and to confirm a purchase. IQarus itself sends nothing anywhere.

Data collected

None. IQarus has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs that collect data. We (the developer) receive nothing from your device, ever.

Data stored on your device

IQarus keeps your progress and settings on your phone, solely so the app can work:

  • Your settings: theme, text size, reduced motion, sound effects, automatic pronunciation, and reminder time and frequency.
  • Your word preferences: the levels, origins, word types and categories you favour or exclude.
  • Your collections, including the names you give them, and the words in each.
  • The words you note each day, and your daily streak.
  • A list of recently shown words, so the feed doesn't repeat itself.
  • Your training records (best score and times played for each game), the counters that unlock achievements, and the title and emblem you've chosen.
  • The date your free week began, and whether you have unlocked the app.

IQarus also keeps a working copy of its bundled dictionary on your phone. It contains nothing about you.

Backups and moving to a new phone

If Android backup is turned on for your phone, Android may include your IQarus progress and settings in your device backup, which Google stores in your own Google account under Google's privacy policy. The same data can be copied directly when you move to a new phone. These are features of Android that you control in your phone's settings. The developer has no access to them. The dictionary copy is excluded from backups.

Deleting your data

  • One collection: on the Profile tab, hold a shelf and choose Clear shelf.
  • Everything: in your phone's Settings, open Apps, then IQarus, then Storage, and choose Clear storage. Uninstalling IQarus also deletes everything it stored.
  • Backups: any copy in your Google account backup is managed in your phone's or Google account's backup settings.

Because we hold none of your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. Your purchase belongs to your Google account, not to the app's storage: after reinstalling, choose I already bought it to restore it.

Pronunciation

Tap-to-hear and automatic pronunciation use the text-to-speech engine installed on your phone, such as Google's speech services. IQarus passes the engine only the dictionary word to be spoken. The engine is part of your phone's software, and its provider's own privacy policy applies to it.

Reminders

Daily word reminders are scheduled and created entirely on your phone. Nothing about them is sent anywhere.

Data shared with third parties

None. IQarus has no connection to any server of ours, because there is no server of ours. It contains no ads.

Purchases

The one-time unlock is sold and processed entirely by Google Play. IQarus never sees your name, email address, payment card or billing address. Google Play only tells the app whether this Google account has bought the unlock, so the app can open for you. Your payment is covered by the Google Play Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Permissions

  • Notifications — to show your daily word reminders. This is the only permission IQarus asks you for, and reminders can be turned off in Settings.
  • Background scheduling — Android's WorkManager component, which IQarus uses to deliver reminders, declares standard permissions such as restoring reminders after your phone restarts. None of them gives access to your personal data.
  • Google Play billing — Google Play's billing library adds internet access and Google Play's billing permission, so the unlock can be bought through Google Play. IQarus uses them for nothing else.

Children

IQarus collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
